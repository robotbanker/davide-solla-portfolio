Rights and scope

A licence is shaped around the intended use.

When enquiring, identify the photograph, publication or brand, media, territories, duration, placement and expected audience. That context allows the studio to confirm availability and agree an appropriate scope. Possession of an image file does not itself grant permission to reproduce, adapt, distribute or publish it.

Commissioned work

Commission usage follows the written agreement.

For a commissioned shoot, permitted usage is recorded in the project agreement or licence. Uses outside that scope require written approval before publication. Credits should read “Davide Solla” unless a project agreement specifies another form.
